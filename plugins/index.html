How ordering works

  1. Email kitebyte.studio@gmail.com with your idea and your server version, for example Paper 1.21.4. Describe what players type or do and what should happen. We reply with the package that fits and any questions.
  2. Once we agree on what the plugin does, you pay through a secure Creem checkout link. Creem handles the payment and sends your receipt.
  3. We build the plugin, test it on a live Paper server and email you the .jar file, the source code and short setup notes.

Questions

Do I get the source code?

Yes, with every package. The plugin and its code are yours.

Which versions do you support?

Paper and Spigot 1.20 and newer, including 26.x. Tell us your exact version.

Do you use AI?

Yes. The code is written with AI coding tools, then compiled and tested on a real Paper server before delivery.

What will you not build?

Copies of paid plugins, cheats or exploits, and anything that breaks the Minecraft EULA or another server's rules.

What if it does not work?

Bugs in what we agreed are fixed for free for 14 days after delivery. If we cannot deliver, you get your money back. See the refund policy.
