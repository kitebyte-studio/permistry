Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Short version: the checker does not upload your files, we use no analytics and no cookies, and we only see the data you send us yourself.

The checker

Everything you paste or drop into Permistry, including plugin jars, is read and checked inside your browser. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses when pages are requested; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not add analytics, tracking pixels or cookies.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Creem, our merchant of record. Creem collects the details needed to process the payment, such as your name, email, country and card data, under its own privacy policy. We receive your email address, the product you bought and the order details so we can deliver it and support you. We never see your full card number.

Email

If you email us, we keep the conversation to answer you and to deliver what you ordered. We do not add you to mailing lists and do not sell or share your data.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the data we hold about you: support@kitebytestudio.com.
